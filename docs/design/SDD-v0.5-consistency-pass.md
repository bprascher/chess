## 2 Result in one line

Store, domain and app (0.4.1) carry Amendment 8 and addenda A to D faithfully: journal fields, kind codes, CRC, text forms, reason codes, DDL CHECKs, `custom_start`, `sha256:v1`, `ReplayItem`/`MoveCheck`, `record_to_replay_item`, `seed_from_facts`, `GameContextProvider` and the `JournalDegraded` command set all agree with the architecture. The defects are at the edges of that work: the **front-end SDDs (CLI, UI, Tauri) and the server were never carried to Amendment 8**, and the **architecture's own fitness-function rows (FF-3, FF-4) disagree with D-67, D-68 and section 11.4.6**. **Verdict: PASS WITH FINDINGS. 2 Major, 6 Minor.**
