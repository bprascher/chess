## 1 Method and limits

- `[RAN]` grep and a Python term/ID scanner over the nine SDDs, `architecture.md` and `requirements.md`, then targeted reads of the hit regions. The SDDs were **not read end to end**; a defect outside the hit regions can be missed.
- Line numbers are those of the working tree on 2026-11-01 (the files are uncommitted).
- Test-ID check `[RAN]`: every `UT-`, `CT-`, `E2E-`, `BM-`, `FZ-` reference in the nine SDDs was compared with the IDs defined at the start of a table row (ranges expanded). A range such as `CT-EN-001..021` was accepted as written; members of a range that are not defined are listed in FIND-JR3-009 and -013 only where the range is the sole reference.
- Not checked: Mermaid validity of the edited diagrams (not machine-validated); `test-strategy.md`; use cases; store DDL against architecture 7.2 column by column; any `ESTIMATE` number beyond consistency.
- Severity: **Major** changes or breaks an interface, a stored format, a public command or a decision text; **Minor** needs a statement, a name or a reference. Gate tier is that of the proposed *fix* (CLAUDE.md, SWE-001 section 3).
